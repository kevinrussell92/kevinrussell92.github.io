About KEV Charging

KEV Charging provides independent consultancy and engineering services within the EV charging industry. We support manufacturers, charge point operators, installers, fleet operators and businesses with technical assessments, system integration, troubleshooting and charging network optimisation.

Services

Technical Consultancy

Advice on charger selection, deployment, maintenance and infrastructure planning.

OCPP & Backend Integration

Support with OCPP communication, charger connectivity and backend migration projects.

Troubleshooting & Diagnostics

Investigation of charging failures, communication issues and payment terminal integration problems.

Compliance & Security

Guidance on industry best practices, connectivity security and operational procedures.
